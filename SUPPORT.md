# Getting Support

Thank you for using `EricksonLopez.Result`! Here are the official channels for getting help and reporting issues.

---

## 💬 Community Support & Q&A

For general usage questions, architectural advice, design discussions, or best practices:

- **[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/ericksonlopezf/dotnet-result/discussions)** — Search existing topics or ask a new question.

---

## 🐛 Bug Reports & Feature Requests

If you encountered a bug or have a concrete feature request:

- **[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ericksonlopezf/dotnet-result/issues)** — Search existing issues or open a new issue.
- Please provide a minimal reproducible sample when reporting bugs.

---

## 🔒 Security Issues

For security vulnerabilities, please refer to our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) and report via email to [ericksonlopezf@gmail.com](mailto:ericksonlopezf@gmail.com) rather than public issues.
